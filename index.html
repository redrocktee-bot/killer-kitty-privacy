Privacy info — The Final Dayz

What this bot records, who can see it, and how to ask for it to be deleted. This is the same notice players see with /privacyinfo in Discord, for anyone who plays on the server without using our Discord.

Last updated November 22, 2026

Where the information comes from

  • Discord: your Discord ID and name, the bot commands you use, and what you type into the bot's forms.
  • The game server's log (from the game host, Nitrado): your PSN name, when you join and leave, where you are on the map, kills and deaths (with weapon and distance), emotes, and building. This is recorded for everyone who plays on the server, even if you never join this Discord or use /register.
  • Staff: bans and their reasons, base approvals.

What the bot keeps

  • Your Discord ID linked to your PSN (after /register), and your verification attempts.
  • Game stats by PSN: play time, kills, deaths, longest kill, life length, distance, kill streaks, deaths to zombies, last time seen, and how long you were online each hour (last 14 days, for a daily staff summary).
  • Your map positions.
  • Player kills and building from the server log (who, what, where, when), kept 7 days for staff to check reports and ban appeals.
  • Bot money: wallet, bank, and a record of every payment, transfer and casino bet, plus shop orders and where they were delivered.
  • Tickets: the type, subject and dates, decisions made in the ticket and staff notes (some staff-only). What you write in a ticket goes to staff in Discord.
  • Bases: location and the PSN names listed.
  • Factions: name, members and bank history.
  • Supply drops: the PSN of the first player to reach each drop.
  • Scavenger hunts: each player's progress by PSN, finishing place and prize.
  • Chicken toss: each throw (distance, angle, power, time) by Discord ID.
  • PvE strikes and bans.

What it doesn't keep

The text of your normal Discord messages, your real name, your IP address, or any payment details. Only what you type into the bot's commands and forms is used.

What other people can see

  • Anyone in this Discord can look up: your game stats (/stats), your bot money (/balance), which PSN belongs to which Discord member (/whois), who is online right now (/server status), faction members and their PSNs (/faction info), how many PvE strikes a PSN has (/strikes view).
  • Posted in channels: when you join and leave; the killfeed (kills and deaths, weapon, distance); the PSN of the first player to reach a supply drop; the PSN of players who finish a scavenger hunt; leaderboards (/top, /chicken top), money transfers and casino and chicken toss results, in the channel where they're used.
  • Staff only: tickets, bans, strikes and registrations, every build and where it was placed, and flagged builds, shop purchases, where players are and have been (last 7 days), for a player report: the reported player's kills, building and positions around that time.
  • Base alerts: if a base has alerts on, its owner, members and faction channel are told the PSN of anyone who comes within 150 m.

Messages the bot sends you

Direct messages about registration, your starting balance, shop deliveries, scavenger hunt clues and prizes, base alerts, declined base applications, a summary when your ticket closes (results and staff notes), inactive-base reminders, faction invites, PvE strikes, an automatic ban for building in a protected area.

Who else handles it

  • The bot's host stores its database, and short-term error logs that can include PSN names.
  • Nitrado (game host) supplies the server log and receives the in-game ban list (PSN names) and shop delivery files.
  • Discord stores everything the bot posts and sends.
  • Backups: a copy of the database is saved every night outside the host and kept for 14 days.
  • Nothing is sold or used for advertising.

How long it's kept

  • Map positions: about 7 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Everything else has no automatic expiry right now: stats, money and transaction history, shop orders and delivery spots, bases, faction history, strikes, verification attempts and ticket records stay until they're deleted.
  • Messages the bot posted in channels stay until staff delete them.
  • If a feature is switched off, the records it already made are still kept until they're deleted.

Deleting your data

Ask a staff member by DM. Include your PSN and/or Discord name. Requests are handled by hand by the server owner; you'll be told what was removed and which copies remain.
